Terminal Experience Studio

Bring your command line to life.

A friendly Linux desktop app that customises your terminal without editing config files. Live previews, one-click Apply, and Undo for everything.

View on GitHub Download v1.0.0

Features

✨ Welcome & MOTD

Choose how your terminal greets you. Write a greeting, pick a mood, add tips, and see a live preview.

🎨 Terminal Appearance

10 theme presets, colors, fonts, cursor shapes, and opacity. Adapters for GNOME Terminal, Kitty, Alacritty, and Konsole.

⚡ Prompt Designer

Pick a prompt style with a live preview. Minimal, Classic, and Two-line work in plain Bash. Starship support included.

🖼️ ASCII Art Studio

Turn any picture into text art with live controls. Conversion happens entirely on your computer.

🤖 AI Companion

Offline-first AI companion with optional Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter, and Ollama support. No internet required at startup.

🛡️ Backups & Recovery

Every change creates a timestamped backup. Restore any backup, remove everything, or switch between dark and light mode.
